# Texture Mapped Projection
Describes a projection for the Viewport that is a flat projection described by simple frustum, which may be asymmetric.

## Parameters
<Parameter name="type" type="string">
Defines the type of this projection. To use the Texture-Mapped Projection, this value has to be `TextureMappedProjection`.
</Parameter>

<Parameter name="fov" type="Object">
This object describes the field of view used by the camera in this planar projection. The field of view can be specified in one of two ways: either symmetrically through `hfov` and `vfov`, or asymmetrically through `left`, `right`, `down`, and `up`.

  <Parameter name="hfov" type="number" optional>The angle (in degrees) covered by the camera in the horizontal direction. This is a symmetric frustum around the center and provides the same result as providing the `left` and `right` values with the half-angle. Must be used together with `vfov`.</Parameter>
  <Parameter name="vfov" type="number" optional>The angle (in degrees) covered by the camera in the vertical direction. This is a symmetric frustum around the center and provides the same result as providing the `up` and `down` values with the half-angle. Must be used together with `hfov`.</Parameter>

  <Parameter name="left" type="number" optional>The angle (in degrees) that is covered by the camera between the central point and the left border of the viewport. The `left` and `right` angles added together are the horizontal field of view of the viewport.</Parameter>
  <Parameter name="right" type="number" optional>The angle (in degrees) that is covered by the camera between the central point and the right border of the viewport. The `left` and `right` angles added together are the horizontal field of view of the viewport.</Parameter>
  <Parameter name="down" type="number" optional>The angle (in degrees) that is covered by the camera between the central point and the bottom border of the viewport. The `down` and `up` angles added together are the vertical field of view of the viewport.</Parameter>
  <Parameter name="up" type="number" optional>The angle (in degrees) that is covered by the camera between the central point and the top border of the viewport. The `down` and `up` angles added together are the vertical field of view of the viewport.</Parameter>
</Parameter>

<Parameter name="distance" type="number" optional default="10.0">
The distance (in meters) at which the virtual render plane is placed. This value is only important when rendering this viewport using stereoscopy as the `distance` and the user's `eyeseparation` are used to compute the change in frustum between the left and the right eyes.
</Parameter>

<Parameter name="orientation" type={<Link to="/docs/reference/configuration/basetypes#orientation">Orientation</Link>} optional>
Describes a fixed orientation for the virtual image plane. This can be provided either as Euler angles or as a quaternion.
</Parameter>

<Parameter name="offset" type={<Link to="/docs/reference/configuration/basetypes#vec3">vec3</Link>} optional default="0,0,0">
A linear offset in meters that is added to the virtual image plane. Must define the three values `x`, `y`, and `z`.
</Parameter>
